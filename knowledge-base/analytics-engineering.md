# Analytics Engineering: From Questions to Decisions

Good dashboards start with business definitions — not charts.

## Recommended workflow

1. **Decision:** what action will change based on this metric?
2. **Grain:** one row per order, route, visit, employee, or day?
3. **Lineage:** which events and dimensions generate the indicator?
4. **Definitions:** denominator, exceptions, time windows, geographies, and cohort boundaries.
5. **Model:** dimensional or semantic layer with transparent calculations.
6. **Validation:** compare against source extracts, sample cases, and edge conditions.
7. **Delivery:** dashboard with sensible filters, performance, accessibility, and an interpretation guide.

## Example portfolio themes (synthetic)

- Route coverage and service frequency
- Operational incidents and resolution times
- Revenue, costs, variance, and business unit performance
- Geographic aggregation and quality checks
